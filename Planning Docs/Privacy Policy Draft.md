# Do & Due Privacy Policy Draft

_Last updated: August 16, 2026_

This is draft copy for the public privacy policy URL required before submitting Do & Due to the App Store. Review and update it before publishing.

## Overview

Do & Due is a personal task and reminder app. It is designed to store your tasks, due dates, recurrence settings, completion history, reminders, and widget data on your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Do & Due does not currently collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Do & Due does not currently use:

* User accounts
* Advertising
* Analytics SDKs
* Tracking technologies
* Third-party data brokers
* Cloud sync
* Server-side task storage

## Data Stored On Your Device

Do & Due stores the information you enter into the app on your device, including:

* Task titles
* Task notes
* Due dates
* Reminder settings
* Recurrence settings
* Completion history

This data is stored locally using Apple's system storage frameworks.

## Widgets and Notifications

Do & Due may write a lightweight task snapshot to a shared on-device App Group container so its widget can display your current tasks.

If you enable reminders, Do & Due schedules local notifications on your device. Notification content may include task titles so you can understand what needs your attention.

Widget data and local notification data are used to provide app functionality on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

## Permissions

Do & Due may ask for notification permission so it can send reminder alerts. You can change notification permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Children's Privacy

Do & Due is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If Do & Due's data practices change, this policy will be updated. Examples of changes that would require an update include adding cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party services.
