## Common Questions

### Notifications are not appearing

Check these items:

* Open the Settings app.
* Find Do & Due.
* Confirm Notifications are allowed.
* Confirm the task has a due date and reminder selected.
* Confirm the reminder time is still in the future.

Do & Due sends reminder notifications locally on your device. Reminders set for the same date and minute may be grouped into one digest notification.

### The widget is not updating

Try these steps:

* Open Do & Due once to refresh the widget snapshot.
* Confirm the task appears in Today, Overdue, or Anytime.
* Remove and re-add the widget if iOS has not refreshed it yet.

Home Screen widgets are glanceable snapshots and are not scrollable lists.

### Completing a task from the widget

On the medium widget, tap the completion circle to complete a task. The widget removes the row immediately. Do & Due applies the real completion the next time the app opens or returns to the foreground.

Tap the task text instead of the circle to open the task in Do & Due.
